### Settings written by Radio Setup (Andy's phone, 2026-10-29 14:47 PDT)

Same on both radios:

- Range setting **`MC_SF10_BW250.0_CR5`** (SF10, 250 kHz, CR 4/5; the
  ladder step that matches the MeshCore community "USA" modulation).
- Frequency **918.000 MHz**. Not the community USA frequency (910.525):
  918.000 is one of the three frequencies the firmware allows client
  repeat on, and the base needs repeat. So the pair is *not* on the public
  MeshCore mesh.
- TX power **22 dBm**.
- Channel **"MeshTest"** in a non-zero channel slot, key = first 16 bytes
  of Andy's phone's `test_channel_psk`. **Matt's phone has a different
  key** (each phone generates its own, once), see "What Matt must do".
- Base only: client repeat **on**; the "beacon" is `trace`, 30 s: the
  mobile traces the base every 30 s (pulled), nothing is written to the
  base for it.

### The one session so far

Session 33 "try 1", map 12, 2026-10-29 14:49 → 18:22 PDT (3 h 33 min), base
`!93b9`, 630 packets, ping 60 s, trace beacon 30 s, radio-measured air
util TX 0.68 %, channel util 1.7 % (max over the session). Rigs: mobile
"T1K" (Seeed T1000-E), base "Seeed L1" (Wio Tracker L1), no antenna
details.

Worth knowing: during that session **222 of 426 traces timed out** (52 %,
in runs of 5–9; loss tracked the SNR, from 30 % at +8 dB to 90 % at −15 dB,
and was the same for both legs of each minute, so it was the radio link at
the edge of coverage, not the app). The out-of-range monitor flipped
**33 times** on it, because its 90 s window fired after two misses and
one lucky answer put it back in range. Fixed on 2026-10-30 (after build
8): the window now spans three misses plus the reply time (120 s for 30 s
traces), coming back needs two answers, and a repeat within five minutes
of coming back is silent. On build 8 Matt will still hear the alarm
every few minutes when the link is marginal.

## What Matt must do on his phone

1. Install from TestFlight (build 8 or later, "Beta Testers" group).
   TestFlight installs are Pro, so MeshCore radios aren't paywalled.
2. **Run Radio Setup with both radios** (Settings → Radio Setup), even
   though the radios already work together: the app only lets a session
   start when *his phone* knows which radio is the base, and roles are
   assigned only by Radio Setup. It will rewrite the MeshTest channel with
   his phone's key on both radios (fine), keep 918.000 / SF10 / 22 dBm
   unless he changes them, and ask which radio is the base: the **L1 is
   the base**, the T1000-E the mobile. Both must be MeshCore (the app
   refuses a mixed pair). No reboot on MeshCore; it takes under a minute
   per radio.
3. Record: leave the L1 somewhere, walk with the T1000-E and the phone,
   Live tab → start a session.

## Troubleshooting by symptom

- **Pairing prompt**: 123456. If iOS never prompts and the connect
  spins, the phone may hold a stale bond: iPhone Settings → Bluetooth →
  ⓘ next to the radio → Forget This Device, then connect again.
- **"Failed to encrypt the connection" / reconnect loop** (seen on Andy's
  phone on Oct 29 after the link dropped, and again today with a
  Meshtastic radio): same fix, Forget This Device; a power cycle of the
  radio helps too. nRF52 radios (both of these) keep Bluetooth on while
  USB is plugged in, unlike the Heltec.
- **Radio not found in the connect list**: T1000-E has no screen; hold
  the button ~3 s to power it on (LED), and check it isn't still paired to
  another phone (MeshCore allows one BLE client at a time).
- **"On the public channel" refusal at session start**: Radio Setup
  wasn't run on that radio, or someone put it back on a community preset
  with the MeshCore app. Re-run Radio Setup.
- **Base shows as absent / beacon off**: the mobile's traces aren't
  answered. Check the L1 is powered, on 918.000 with repeat on (Radio
  Setup → base), and within range; a trace needs both legs.
- **Radio Setup fails on the frequency**: the base's repeat flag is only
  accepted at 433.000, 869.495 or 918.000 MHz; the app prefills 918.000.
- **Getting the log off Matt's phone**: Settings → Data has the diag log
  and CSV exports; he can share them from the app. On a Mac with the
  phone cabled: `xcrun devicectl device copy from --device <udid>
  --domain-type appDataContainer --domain-identifier
  com.andybrunker.meshAnalyzer --source Documents/diag.log --destination
  ./diag.log` (also `Documents/mesh_analyzer.sqlite`).

## Putting a radio back to Meshtastic

Double-tap reset (or the UF2 bootloader button) mounts `T1000-E` /
`TRACKER L1` as a drive; copy the Meshtastic `.uf2` onto it (the copy
"fails" with "Device not configured" as the board reboots: that is
success). Then Radio Setup from scratch. The original Meshtastic settings
are in the app's Radio Backups on Andy's phone: `292c202a_2026-10-24…
.mabackup` (L1, LONG_FAST US, default channel, firmware 2.7.15) and
`13afe4ea_2026-10-16…json` / `73eed5b5_2026-10-23…json` (T1000-E).

## What could not be collected from the Mac

- Anything over USB: the BLE companion build is silent on serial, and
  `meshcore-cli` can't be run from this agent (blocked), nor can `bleak`
  scan from a shell (no Bluetooth permission). The settings above come
  from the app's preferences, database and diag log on Andy's phone.
- The L1 was not on the bench; its row is from the phone's data.
- A MeshCore `.mabackup` for either radio: the phone's build predates
  that feature (added 2026-10-29 evening), so the only "original" MeshCore
  state is the firmware's defaults after flashing.
